# Listening and Active Communication

## Question 1: What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

- Focus on the speaker and topic instead rather than getting distracted by your own thoughts.
- Try not to interrupt the other person. Let them finish and then respond.
- Use door openers to show that you are interested.
- Use body language to show that you are listening.
- If appropriate take notes during conversation.
- Be on the same page with the speaker.

---

## Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

- Pay full attention to what other is saying.
- Try to understand what other is really trying to say.
- Ask questions rather than making assumptions.
- Repeat the main idea in your own words.
- Understand their point of view and than give your own opinion.

---

- I start preparing what I want to say instead of fully listening.
- I starts assuming rather than listening completely.
- When the discussion is lengthy, I often lose focus.
- I get distracted with the surroundings.

## Question 4: What can you do to improve your listening?

- I will pay attention to what speaker is saying.
- I will listen completely rather than assuming.
- I will ask doubt rather than making assumptions.
- I will pay attention to speaker's tone and expression rather than just words.

## Question 5: When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?

- I switch to passive communication when I am worried about upsetting someone.
- I do it when I fear being judged.
- I do it when I feel that my needs are less important than the other's person

---

## Question 6: When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

- I switch to aggressive communication when I feel angry or frustrated.
- When I feel other person is not listening to me.
- This happens when my emotions become strong and I reacted rudely instead of taking a moment to calm down.

---

## Question 7: When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

- I switch to passive aggressive communication when I am upset with someone but do not feel comfortable expressing my feelings.
- This happens when I want the other person to know that I am unhappy but avoiding direct confrontation.
- I am improving this by speaking openly about what I feel and need.
