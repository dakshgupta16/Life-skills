# Focus Management

## Question 1: What is Deep Work?

Deep Work is focusing on the mentally demanding task without getting distracted.

---

## Question 2: According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?

- Distractions should be avoided
- Deep work requires sustained focus for at least one hour
- Implement a daily Routine

---

## Question 3: How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?

- I will put my phone away while doing Deep work
- I will do an evening shutdown
- I will write down plan for next day at the end of the day to completely disconnect and rest.

---

## Question 4: What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

- Social Media damages focus and concentration.
- It triggers Mental Health issues like loneliness, isolation, etc.
- It spikes sudden anxiety.
