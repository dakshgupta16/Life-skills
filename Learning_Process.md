# Learning Process

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? (1 line)

The Feynman Technique means learning something by explaining it in very simple words as if I am teaching it to someone.

---

## Question 2: What was the most interesting story or idea for you?

I liked Barbara Oakley's story the most. She was not good at maths at first, but later she became an engineer. It made me feel that anyone can improve if they keep practicing and use the correct way of learning.

---

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Active Mode:** When I am fully focused on studying or solving a problem.
- **Diffused Mode:** When my mind is relaxed, like during a walk or break, and it helps me connect ideas.

---

## Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when learning a new topic?

- Divide the topic into small parts.
- Learn from mistakes and improve them.
- Stay away from distractions.
- Keep practicing regularly for around 20 hours.

## Question 5: What actions can you take to improve your learning process?

- I will explain topics in my own words after learning them.
- I will study one step at a time instead of rushing.
- I will keep my phone away while studying.
- I will practice coding every day.
- I will check my progress every week.
- I will ask doubts whenever I get confused.
- I will make small projects to improve my skills.
