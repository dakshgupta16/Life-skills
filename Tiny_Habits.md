## Question 2: How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.

- M,A and P stands for Motivation, Ability and Prompt.
- Motivation is the desire to do something.
- Ability is how easy or difficult it is for you to perform any behavior.
- Prompt is the trigger.
- Shrink the habit into the tiny habit so that we can get enough motivation, ability and trigger to do it.
- Even if we are tired, we can do these tiny habits which is like nurturing a seed until it get its root.
